Introduction Manual data entry is time-consuming and increases the risk of errors. Business expenses are often a hidden culprit of this inefficiency, requiring multiple people to enter data to process a single expense. OCR and Transactional Data are two options used to automate this data entry and can save companies up to 20 minutes per expense report.
